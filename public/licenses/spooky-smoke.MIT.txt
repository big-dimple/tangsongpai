Spooky Smoke rnd/noise/fBm adaptation
Source: https://github.com/pulkitxm/claude-directory/blob/f3d7e12f34bf7d90130dce3ec3b26cf69c29794e/shaders/spooky-smoke-shader/src/components/ui/spooky-smoke-animation.tsx
License: https://github.com/pulkitxm/claude-directory/blob/f3d7e12f34bf7d90130dce3ec3b26cf69c29794e/LICENSE
Adaptation: rnd/noise/fBm math in a cached64x32 CPU wake and a native Phaser Shader with live two/three-octave domain warping, premultiplied density alpha, card-exterior/foreign-print masks and bounded framebuffers. Original React/WebGL renderer, opaque background, DPR sizing and RAF loop are not included.
